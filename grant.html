To Whom It May Concern,

Cheap Chariots Transportation (CCT) is requesting grant support to expand affordable, clean, and sustainable transportation services in our communities. With grant funding, we will be able to:

Expand the fleet

Grow our fleet of Tesla electric vehicles.

Reach more neighborhoods

Increase service coverage in underserved areas.

Create local jobs

Create job opportunities for local drivers.

Support clean energy

Advance environmental goals and affordable rides for those who need them most.

We appreciate your consideration and support in helping us build a cleaner, healthier, and more connected future.
